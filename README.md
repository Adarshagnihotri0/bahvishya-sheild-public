# Bhavishya

**Bhavishya stops your private details from slipping into AI chats.** Card numbers, Aadhaar and PAN,
passwords, API keys, phone numbers and more are caught before they reach ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and
other AI apps. Pip, a small friendly helper, tells you what he caught and lets you decide.
Everything stays on your Mac.

## ⬇️ Download

### [Download Bhavishya for Mac](https://github.com/Adarshagnihotri0/bahvishya-sheild-public/releases/latest/download/Bhavishya.dmg)

Works on any recent Mac with Google Chrome.

## Install in 3 steps

1. **Open** the downloaded `Bhavishya.dmg`.
2. **Double-click "Install Bhavishya".**
   If your Mac says it can't check the app: right-click "Install Bhavishya" → **Open** → **Open**.
   If it asks to install "command line developer tools", click **Install** and wait, then double-click
   "Install Bhavishya" again.
3. **Add the Chrome helper (once).** Chrome opens on its extensions page and Bhavishya shows you how:
   switch on **Developer mode** (top right) → **Load unpacked** → press **⌘⇧G**, then **⌘V**, then
   **Enter** → **Select**.

That's it. Pip says hi on chatgpt.com when everything works. Bhavishya keeps itself up to date.
